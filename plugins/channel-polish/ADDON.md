Replaces fancy Unicode characters in channel names, so they read and search like plain text. Your channels are not renamed: only what you see changes.

`𝑳𝒊𝒌𝒆-𝑻𝒉𝒊𝒔` → `Like-This`

A channel shows its original name while its settings are open.

## Settings

Each kind of channel has its own options: text channels, voice channels, forums, media channels, categories, announcement threads, public threads, private threads and group DMs.

- **Replace fancy Unicode**. Turns decorative letters into plain ones and removes decorative symbols. Emoji stay. On by default.
- **Replace hyphens with spaces**. Off by default.
- **Letter case**. Unchanged (default), title case (only the first word: `Hello world`) or capitalize each word (`Hello World`).

Other channels, such as announcement and stage channels, only have fancy Unicode replaced.

**Replace fancy Unicode** keeps Latin letters, currency symbols and emoji only: it removes other scripts, such as Cyrillic, Greek or Japanese. Turn it off if your channels use them.

Do not enable this plugin together with another one that rewrites channel names.
